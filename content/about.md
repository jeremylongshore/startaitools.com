<p class="about-value-prop"><strong>Start AI Tools is an implementation journal and knowledge center that publishes operator-grade write-ups on shipping AI, DevOps, and production systems for builders and operators who need implementation notes rather than tool-directory summaries.</strong></p>

The site records what was built, what failed, what the evidence showed, and what changed next. It is presented by [Intent Solutions](https://intentsolutions.io/about/) and grounded in active production work.

## What Start AI Tools does

### Publishes field notes

Field notes capture a bounded implementation decision, failure, or repair close to the day it happened. They give operators a concise record they can compare with their own systems without turning the event into a generic tutorial.

### Produces technical deep-dives

Deep-dives explain the architecture, constraints, tests, and operational tradeoffs behind a shipped system. Readers get enough implementation detail to evaluate the approach and adapt the useful parts to their own environment.

### Documents case studies

Case studies connect a business or engineering problem to the system that was built, the evidence used to judge it, and the limits of the result. They show the work as an operating system rather than a finished screenshot.

### Maintains practitioner references

Features, research pages, monthly retrospectives, and ecosystem guides organize related work beyond the daily journal. These references give practitioners a durable route from one implementation note to the broader technical context.

## What makes Start AI Tools different

### The record is dated

Each journal entry carries a publication date and sits in a chronological archive. Readers can distinguish what was known at the time from a later correction or follow-up.

### The evidence is inspectable

Posts link to repositories, pull requests, tests, runbooks, measurements, and public artifacts when those sources can be shared. Claims are scoped to the evidence available instead of being generalized into universal advice.

### Production constraints stay in the story

The writing covers deployment, rollback, observability, access, cost, and ownership alongside model behavior. That makes the archive useful to the people who have to operate the system after a demo succeeds.

### The editorial tiers match the work

The site separates short field notes, technical deep-dives, case studies, and longer research. Each tier has its own review gates, so a small operational lesson does not need to pretend to be a research paper and a large claim does not ship with field-note evidence.

### Corrections remain visible

Follow-up posts and retrospectives preserve the sequence from initial decision to later repair. The archive is intended to show how the system changed, including cases where an earlier assumption or green check turned out to be incomplete.

## Who uses Start AI Tools

- AI engineers moving agent, RAG, evaluation, or model-integration work into production.
- DevOps and platform engineers responsible for deployment, observability, recovery, and access controls.
- Technical founders who need concrete implementation patterns before committing a team or budget.
- Operators evaluating what it takes to own an AI workflow after launch.
- Maintainers building skills, plugins, hooks, MCP integrations, and agent tooling in public repositories.

## How Start AI Tools works

### Read without an account

The public archive is open to read, and no onboarding or account is required. Start with the latest posts, a topic tag, a feature, or a monthly retrospective, then follow the linked evidence when you need more depth.

### Follow the publishing cadence

New work is produced through a governed pipeline that checks content structure, supporting evidence, voice, links, and the Hugo build before publication. The site publishes on a daily operating cadence when there is completed work worth recording; it does not guarantee a new article every day.

### Contact the person doing the work

Questions and engagement requests go to [jeremy@intentsolutions.io](mailto:jeremy@intentsolutions.io) or [support@intentsolutions.io](mailto:support@intentsolutions.io), and the existing response target is within 24 hours. Readers and prospective customers work directly with Jeremy; project onboarding and delivery timing are set after the outcome, current system, evidence, and deadline are understood.

### Inspect the source trail

Use the links inside an article to reach the corresponding repository, pull request, demo, or reference when it is public. If evidence cannot be published because it contains customer or private operating data, the article must limit the claim rather than imply that the evidence is public.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Start AI Tools, exactly?

Start AI Tools is the public implementation journal and knowledge center presented by Intent Solutions. It publishes field notes, deep-dives, case studies, and references drawn from the work of building and operating production systems.

### Who is Start AI Tools for?

It is for builders and operators who want architecture, failure modes, tests, and operating evidence they can inspect. It is not designed as a broad consumer tool directory or a feed of vendor announcements.

### How is Start AI Tools different from [[NAMED_ALTERNATIVE]]?

Start AI Tools is organized around dated implementation records and the evidence behind them. A factual comparison with a specific alternative still needs a verified name and public source before publication.

### How do I start or onboard?

Reading is self-serve and requires no account, so start with the latest post or a topic relevant to the system you are building. For an Intent Solutions engagement, email the outcome, current environment, known failures, and deadline; Jeremy responds within 24 hours and scopes the next step directly.

### What does it cost and are there contracts?

The publication is free to read and does not require a contract. Pricing and contract terms for implementation work are [[CONSULTING_PRICING_AND_TERMS]].

### How does this relate to Intent Solutions and the other sites?

Intent Solutions is the parent company, and Start AI Tools is its implementation journal. The sibling sites publish learning programs, working demos, desktop plugins, agent skills, and focused products, while this site records how systems are built and operated.

### Where can I inspect the evidence or source?

Follow the repository, pull request, test, demo, citation, or runbook links in each article. The site itself is built from the public [startaitools.com repository](https://github.com/jeremylongshore/startaitools.com), and claims without public supporting material are scoped accordingly.
