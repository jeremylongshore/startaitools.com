Twelve posts on this site mention Intent OS. Not one of them describes it as a system. A reader
who lands here has to read six posts to infer the spine, and the spine itself stays invisible.
This feature is the spine.

Intent Solutions is one operator and a fleet of agents. The estate they govern is real and
countable: 191 repositories in the registry, 254 service entities, 46 containers, 16 declared
capabilities, 49 beads databases holding 1,125 open issues. It was built between 2026-06-13 and
2026-08-13, which is two months, in 598 commits.

The four chapters are ordered by surprise. The cockpit turns out to be a folder of Markdown with
no server and no database behind it. The operator is often not at a desk, and is talking rather
than typing. The status page is engineered so that showing green without fresh evidence is a
schema violation, not a judgment call. And the output is large enough to be worth counting.

Every number here was re-derived on 2026-08-13 from a command, not copied from an earlier draft.
Where a claim did not survive that check, it was cut rather than softened. One was.

## Chapters

1. [**The Cockpit Is a Folder of Markdown**](/features/running-on-agents/the-cockpit-is-a-folder-of-markdown/)
   Nine generated pages, four cron collectors, no server, no database, no UI, and a write guard
   that keeps a failed generator from ever replacing a good page.
2. [**Dictated From a Phone**](/features/running-on-agents/dictated-from-a-phone/)
   What the loop actually is when the operator is on the move: talk, agents build, deterministic
   gates catch what the agents get wrong, receipts land in the repo.
3. [**The Status Page That Refuses to Lie**](/features/running-on-agents/the-status-page-that-refuses-to-lie/)
   Twenty-two schema conditionals, 80 negative fixtures, and a rule that decides what the word
   operational means.
4. [**Two Months, 598 Commits**](/features/running-on-agents/two-months-598-commits/)
   What one operator plus agents produced, and what it is all for.
